# PUI89 Commercial Product & Company-Readiness Standard

**Portfolio rule:** mandatory for this and every future PUI89 project. Goal: lawful commercial use and an evidence-backed path from research to high-quality products and a future PUI89 company.

## Project-specific gates
This is a legacy HOMEGUARD AI repository. Reconcile scope and ownership with Homeguard-Ai-Nomad; avoid duplicate product claims. Validate building-safety claims and keep certified alarms and qualified professionals authoritative.

## Commercial rights and intellectual property
- The root LICENSE governs original materials the owner is entitled to license. MIT and Apache-2.0 permit commercial use, modification and redistribution subject to their conditions.
- Third-party code, models, datasets, images, CAD, fonts and other assets are not automatically covered. Record source, version, owner, exact licence, attribution, restrictions and commercial-use permission before inclusion or sale.
- Exclude assets with unclear, research-only, non-commercial, no-redistribution or incompatible terms.
- PUI89 names, logos and trademarks are not granted by the software licence. Keep brand permissions separate.
- Maintain contributor permissions, copyright provenance, third-party notices and an SBOM for distributable software.

## First-class product quality
Define customer, intended use and exclusions; measurable requirements and acceptance criteria; architecture; reproducible builds and tests; traceability; secure defaults; privacy/data governance; user, installation, maintenance and troubleshooting documentation; support, incident, rollback and end-of-life plans.

## Safety, security and regulatory gates
Complete hazard/risk assessment, safe-state design, human override and misuse analysis before customer use. For AI, document data provenance, label quality, calibration, uncertainty, error rates and human review. For hardware, define and test mechanical, electrical, thermal, battery, fire, ingress, EMC and environmental requirements. Identify applicable laws, standards and approvals with qualified assessors. Simulation/software tests do not establish physical safety or field performance.

## Commercial stage gates
| Gate | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Concept | Customer problem, intended use, exclusions, risks | Concept |
| G1 Requirements | Requirements, architecture, hazards, IP/data/licence review | Specified |
| G2 Prototype | Working software/prototype and reproducible tests | Prototype |
| G3 Verification | Tests, benchmarks, security review, failure analysis | Verified within scope |
| G4 Validation | Representative trials and independent review where needed | Validated within scope |
| G5 Compliance | Required legal, safety, privacy and market approvals | Approved only with evidence |
| G6 Pilot | Qualified supply, support, warranty and service processes | Pilot |
| G7 Commercial release | Sign-off, unit economics, quality controls and post-market support | Sale-ready only after required gates |

## Startup readiness
Maintain product catalogue, IP/contributor register, third-party licence inventory, customer discovery/pilot records, unit-economics model, supplier qualification, repair/warranty/support plans and separation of open-source code, proprietary components, services and brand assets. Existing open-source grants cannot simply be withdrawn.

## Strict zero-cost rule
Prefer free/open-source and offline/local tools. No paid APIs, subscriptions, cloud GPUs, paid datasets, metered services, automatic paid upgrades or unexpected billing. Check licence, provenance, security, resource fit and cost before adding dependencies/models/data/services. Record unfunded certification, lab testing, legal review or manufacturing as future funding/partner gates; do not incur charges without explicit approval.

## Required records
Create and maintain as the project matures: docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md, docs/HAZARD_AND_RISK_REGISTER.md, docs/VERIFICATION_AND_VALIDATION_PLAN.md, docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and docs/COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md. Their mention does not mean they exist or have passed review.

## Truthful claims
Use PROPOSED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, SIMULATION-ONLY, BENCH-TESTED, FIELD-TESTED, INDEPENDENTLY-VALIDATED and CERTIFIED/APPROVED only when supported by traceable evidence. Never market a concept as finished.

**PUI89 standard:** commercial rights clarity, disciplined engineering, safety-first design, evidence before claims and zero-cost development until funding is explicitly approved.
