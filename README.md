### An Indoor Mobile Assistant for Home Safety, Monitoring and Everyday Support

**A calmer, more understandable view of home conditions.**

PUI89 AI HOME is a dedicated indoor mobile assistant concept focused on home safety, environmental monitoring and everyday support. It uses a compact four-wheel design and remains a separate product from PUI89 AI NOMAD, which is intended for indoor and outdoor mobility, travel and carrying assistance.

![PUI89 AI HOME indoor four-wheel robot concept](https://pui89.github.io/images/homeguard-ai-home-concept.svg)

## Product purpose

HOME is intended to help residents understand changes and possible hazards inside a home or small building. It is a software-first, evidence-led concept; it is not a certified alarm, building inspector or replacement for qualified professionals.

Research areas include:
- Water leaks, moisture and humidity changes
- Smoke, abnormal heat and fire-related warning evidence
- Electrical anomaly indicators, without operating breakers or wiring
- Visible cracks and vibration/change observations, without claiming structural diagnosis
- Mold-risk conditions based on environmental evidence
- Sound, light, temperature and other environmental changes
- Clear reports showing what is known, unknown, changed, uncertain and worth checking

The 8 kg target and 10 kg maximum include the battery, sensors, computer, enclosure and accessories. They are engineering requirements, not measured prototype specifications.

## Shared intelligence, independent products

HOME may reuse versioned PUI89 AI Core contracts and services:
- WorldState, Observation, ActionProposal and FailureReport
- Evidence provenance, freshness and sensor-health checks
- Uncertainty propagation and conflict/missing-data handling
- Human-reviewed recommendations and safety gates
- Reproducible tests and machine-readable reports

HOME and NOMAD must keep separate mechanical designs, navigation, sensor configurations, operating environments, risk assessments and validation evidence.

## Safety and privacy boundaries

- Certified smoke, fire, carbon-monoxide and electrical protection devices remain primary.
- Never delay evacuation or emergency procedures for AI analysis.
- HOME must not operate locks, breakers, valves, cooking appliances or certified alarms.
- Stop when localization, obstacle evidence or battery state is uncertain.
- Do not claim fire detection, structural diagnosis, electrical fault diagnosis or hazard prevention without appropriate evidence and independent validation.
- Minimize personal data and provide clear owner control.

## Security, malware and supply-chain protection

Security policy: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report suspected vulnerabilities privately; do not post resident data, camera footage, access details or exploit steps publicly.

Project-specific controls and review requirements:
- Treat sensor readings, camera/audio inputs, network messages, configuration files and imported logs as untrusted.
- Validate source, timestamps, ranges, file sizes and stale/conflicting readings; fail safely when evidence is unreliable.
- Never commit credentials, resident information, floor plans, private recordings or network secrets.
- Review dependencies, model files and GitHub Actions for provenance, licences and vulnerabilities before adoption.
- AI recommendations must not automatically operate locks, breakers, valves, appliances or emergency systems; retain human authority.
- Use least-privilege CI and never expose secrets to untrusted pull-request code.

A scan or passing test is evidence only for the checks actually run; it does not establish whether the repository contains malware or vulnerabilities.

## Materials & Hardware — Research Plan

**Status: software-first concept and hardware research.** This is not a claim of a built or certified home-safety robot. Start with fixed sensors and a software dashboard before considering mobility.

| Subsystem | Candidate materials / hardware | Purpose and checks before selection |
|---|---|---|
| Housing/mounts | Verified flame-retardant-rated enclosure materials where appropriate, polymers or metal brackets | Verify actual material rating, heat exposure, ventilation and mounting strength |
| Electrical monitoring | Certified plug-in energy meter or professionally installed sensing equipment | Mains isolation, local electrical code, measurement accuracy and qualified installation |
| Temperature/humidity | Digital sensors and protected vents | Calibration, placement, condensation and drift |
| Water/leak | Floor leak probes, rope sensors or contact sensors | Coverage, false alarms, corrosion and battery life |
| Fire/smoke interface | Certified standalone smoke/heat alarm; permitted alarm-state interface only | Local fire code, certification and alarm independence; never replace life-safety alarms |
| Crack/vibration | Contact vibration sensor, accelerometer and optional visual markers | Mounting, baseline, noise and structural-engineer interpretation |
| Air/mould-risk context | Humidity/temperature sensors; optional VOC sensor as a non-diagnostic indicator | Selectivity, calibration and no claim to diagnose mould |
| Sound/light | Sound-event and ambient-light sensors | Privacy, consent, local processing and minimal recording |
| Compute/connectivity | Low-power local computer or microcontroller, local network and offline storage | Power budget, updates, access control and cybersecurity |
| Power/backup | Certified low-voltage supply, protected battery backup and fusing | Electrical safety, battery thermal risk and backup runtime |

Avoid modifying building wiring, fire systems or structural elements without qualified professionals and permissions. Sensors provide indications, not definitive diagnoses of structural damage, fire cause, mould or electrical faults. Physical installation and connections to mains or life-safety systems require qualified installers and applicable approvals.

**Procurement rule:** research only. No purchases, subscriptions, supplier contact or paid services without explicit approval. Verify datasheets, approvals, sensor limits, materials, price, warranty and privacy implications.
